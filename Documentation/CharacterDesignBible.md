# OUTPOST: SURVIVE BEYOND EARTH
## Character Design Bible & Visual Style Guide
**Target Demographic:** Children aged 7–12  
**Genre:** 3D Survival & Educational Simulation  
**Setting:** Lunar South Pole (Shackleton Rim), Year 2045  
**System Ownership:** SYSTEM 2 (3D Characters, Rigging, Models, Materials)  
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  

---

### 1. Visual Philosophy & Art Direction

#### A. Tone & Demographic Appropriateness
The visual design balances **authentic near-future NASA space exploration** with an **approachable, heroic, and inspiring visual style** suitable for young players aged 7–12. 
- **Approachable Realism:** EVA suits are grounded in real Artemis-generation Next-Gen Extravehicular Mobility Units (xEMU), but feature clean lines, readable shapes, and energetic color accents rather than dreary or weathered grime.
- **Immediate Silhouette Readability:** Each crew member must be instantly identifiable from a distance, from behind, and in low-contrast lunar lighting through distinct gear shapes, backpack profiles, and vibrant accent colors.
- **Expressive Faces & Friendly Connection:** Helmets feature clear, anti-reflective poly-carbonate visors showcasing warm, expressive character faces with visible eyes and animated facial cues. Gold sunshields are flipped up during interior and shaded exploration so the player can always connect emotionally with the crew.

#### 1. COMMANDER ELENA
- **Role:** Mission Commander
- **Personality:** Calm, disciplined, responsible, decisive. The steady anchor of the outpost.
- **Demographic Appeal:** Confident leader, protective and encouraging, inspires teamwork.
- **Visual Silhouette & Stance:** Tall, athletic posture, erect shoulders. Confident two-handed stance or arms folded thoughtfully.
- **Color Accent:** **Commander Royal Blue (`#0055B8`)** shoulder cowls, forearm bands, and double gold command piping down the legs.
- **Unique Gear & Accessories:**
  - **Wrist Telemetry Terminal:** High-resolution digital display on left forearm showing outpost overall status.
  - **Command Comm Mast:** Dual slim radio whip antennas extending from the life-support backpack.
  - **Insignia:** Mission Commander patch on left chest; Lunar Artemis 2045 mission emblem on right shoulder.
- **Face & Helmet:**
  - Dark braided hair pinned neatly inside the communications "Snoopy cap".
  - Warm brown eyes, decisive jawline, reassuring smile.
  - Helmet visor: Crystal-clear bubble with soft cyan heads-up display edge glow.

---

#### 2. ENGINEER MARCUS
- **Role:** Systems Engineer
- **Personality:** Practical, confident, technical, occasionally humorous. The master builder and fixer.
- **Demographic Appeal:** The hands-on problem solver. Kids connect with his gadgetry, tool belt, and can-do enthusiasm.
- **Visual Silhouette & Stance:** Broad-shouldered, sturdy build, slightly lowered center of gravity, active hands ready to work.
- **Color Accent:** **Industrial Safety Orange (`#FF6600`)** torso stripes, reinforced knee guards, and hazard hazard-chevron arm patches.
- **Unique Gear & Accessories:**
  - **Heavy-Duty Utility Rig:** Reinforced utility belt equipped with magnetic tether clamps, torque multitool, and diagnostic cable spools.
  - **Dual High-Intensity Work Spotlights:** Twin adjustable LED beam pods mounted directly onto helmet temples for illuminating dark machinery bays.
  - **Reinforced Hard-Armor Plating:** Textured dark titanium-composite scuff plates over knees, elbows, and boots to resist sharp regolith abrasion.
- **Face & Helmet:**
  - Short buzz-cut hair, energetic friendly stubble, warm smile with laugh lines.
  - Quick, observant amber-brown eyes.
  - Helmet visor: Heavy-duty octagonal reinforced collar rim.

---

#### 3. SCIENTIST AISHA
- **Role:** Lunar Scientist
- **Personality:** Curious, intelligent, analytical, observant. Fascinated by discovery and geology.
- **Demographic Appeal:** Sparks wonder about space science, planetary origins, water ice, and lunar mysteries.
- **Visual Silhouette & Stance:** Agile, nimble posture, often leaning forward examining rocks or holding up scanning equipment.
- **Color Accent:** **Research Cyan / Teal (`#00D4D4`)** chest panels, glove cuffs, and glowing scanner lens rings.
- **Unique Gear & Accessories:**
  - **Geological Sample Chest Rack:** Modular vacuum-sealed canisters mounted across the lower chest harness for lunar rock specimens.
  - **Handheld Multi-Spectral Spectrometer:** Articulated sensor wand sheathed on the right thigh for analyzing water-ice, titanium, and helium-3 signatures.
  - **Panoramic Science Visor:** Enlarged, extra-wide observation visor optimized for field research and geological horizon surveying.
- **Face & Helmet:**
  - Hijab-styled aerodynamic under-suit cap framing an animated, deeply intelligent face.
  - Expressive dark hazel eyes wide with genuine scientific curiosity.

---

#### 4. MEDICAL OFFICER DANIEL
- **Role:** Medical Officer
- **Personality:** Compassionate, careful, reassuring. Always watchful of crew vitals, nutrition, and well-being.
- **Demographic Appeal:** Reassuring, gentle presence that eases anxiety during emergency chapters (e.g. life-support failures).
- **Visual Silhouette & Stance:** Gentle, open body language, attentive posture. Often seen checking crew biometrics.
- **Color Accent:** **Medical Emerald Green (`#00B050`)** cross emblems, shoulder bands, and bio-monitor status lights.
- **Unique Gear & Accessories:**
  - **Life-Sign Scanner Wand:** Compact contact-free biometric scanner holstered on left hip.
  - **Environmental Life-Support Backpack (PLSS):** Extended auxiliary filtration canister and crew emergency oxygen reserve manifold.
  - **Medical Caduceus Insignia:** Clean green and silver international space medicine crest on chest.
- **Face & Helmet:**
  - Neatly trimmed wavy dark hair under communications liner.
  - Kind green eyes, calm and reassuring expression.

---

#### 5. ORION (AI ASSISTANT)
- **Full Designation:** Onboard Robotic Intelligence and Operations Network
- **Role:** Outpost AI Assistant, systems monitor, early warning sentinel, and educational science tutor.
- **Visual Style:**
  - **Station Mode:** Wall terminal screens and holographic pedestals displaying a rotating geometric spherical core with glowing concentric orbital rings.
  - **EVA Companion Drone Mode:** A buoyant, anti-gravity floating spherical orb ($0.35\text{m}$ diameter) with a circular cyan lens eye that pulses and dilates in sync with synthesized speech.
- **Aesthetic Color:** **Luminescent Holographic Azure (`#38E1FF`)** with energetic white warning flashes (`#FF3333`) during emergency alerts.
- **Personality:** Friendly, articulate, educational, slightly witty, completely loyal and helpful.

### 3. Technical 3D Asset Guidelines

#### A. Character Mesh Specifications
| Parameter | Standard Specification | WebGL Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Polygon Count (LOD0)** | 8,500 – 10,000 Triangles | Smooth silhouettes without exceeding mobile WebGL memory caps |
| **Polygon Count (LOD1)** | 4,000 – 5,000 Triangles | Switched at $15\text{m}$ distance |
| **Polygon Count (LOD2)** | 1,500 – 2,000 Triangles | Switched at $35\text{m}$; culled beyond $50\text{m}$ |
| **Bone Count** | 56 Bones (Standard Humanoid) | Avoids 4-bone influence CPU bottleneck on low-end WebGL |
| **Skinned Mesh Renderer** | Max 2 skinned mesh sub-materials | Limits draw calls per character to 2 |
| **Materials per Character** | 1 Master PBR Lit Material | Shared 1024x1024 texture set |

#### B. Texture Set per Character (1024 x 1024)
1. `T_Astronaut_[Name]_Albedo.png`: Base color with TMG weave details, mission patches, and accent stripes.
2. `T_Astronaut_[Name]_Normal.png`: Tangent normal containing suit wrinkles, seam stitching, and buckle relief.
3. `T_Astronaut_[Name]_MetallicSmoothness.png`: Channel-packed (R: Metallic, G: AO, A: Smoothness).
4. `T_Astronaut_[Name]_Emission.png`: LED status indicators, chest display text, and helmet headlamp cones.

#### C. Humanoid Animation Set (Mecanim Compatible)
- `Locomotion_LunarIdle`: Low-gravity gentle sway, natural breathing cycle.
- `Locomotion_LunarWalk`: Bounding, low-gravity gait ($1.2\text{m/s}$).
- `Locomotion_LunarJog`: Distinct Apollo-style bounding leap ($3.5\text{m/s}$).
- `Interaction_ConsoleType`: Hand movements across outpost computer terminals.
- `Interaction_InspectRock`: Kneeling down, picking up sample, examining with scanner.
- `Interaction_RepairFilter`: Hands-on two-handed twisting and seating of filter canisters.
- `Emote_EmergencyAlert`: Startled glance upward, steadying posture, tapping helmet radio.
- `Emote_VictoryCelebrate`: Gentle fist pump and friendly visor thumbs-up.
